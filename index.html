CatchUp Pal

Paste a long chat. Get a short summary, every deadline and the tasks that matter. Optional on-device AI with Hugging Face.

Runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  1. Paste a chat or upload an exported .txt file.
  2. Analyze to get a summary, deadlines and tasks.
  3. Act on what is urgent and tick off what is done.

Runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded or saved.
